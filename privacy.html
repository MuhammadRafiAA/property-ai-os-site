Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Property AI OS processes only information required to provide authorized integrations and publishing workflows.

Information processed

When a user connects a third-party platform such as TikTok, the service may process authorization credentials, account identifiers, basic profile information made available by the authorized scope, and publishing-related information.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate authorized accounts, perform requested publishing actions, maintain integration state, and diagnose technical failures.

Data sharing

Personal information is not sold. Information is shared with third-party platforms only as necessary to perform actions explicitly requested through their official APIs.

Security

Authentication credentials and access tokens are treated as sensitive information and are not intended to be publicly exposed.

Data retention and access revocation

Integration data is retained only as necessary for the service. Users may revoke access to connected platforms through the relevant platform settings.

Contact

Privacy questions may be directed to the operator of Property AI OS.
