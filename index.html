Property AI OS

Property AI OS is a property marketing workflow tool that helps authorized real estate agents prepare and publish property marketing content to their connected social media accounts.

What the service does

The service assists with preparing property marketing content, managing publishing workflows, and sending approved content to supported social media platforms through their official APIs.

TikTok integration

TikTok accounts are connected only after the account owner explicitly authorizes access. Content is posted only to accounts authorized by that user.
